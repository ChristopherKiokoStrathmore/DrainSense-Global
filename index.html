DrainSense

Know where floods will hit and which drain to fix first, so limited budgets prevent disasters instead of paying for recovery.

ProblemBlocked drains go unreported, so floods strike without warning and authorities cannot tell which areas to inspect first.
IdeaCombine rainfall, flood history, satellite data and community reports into an explainable risk map that ranks hotspots and tracks repairs.
ValueFewer lives and livelihoods lost, lower emergency costs, and better decisions for residents, authorities, farmers, hospitals, insurers, logistics firms and planners.
